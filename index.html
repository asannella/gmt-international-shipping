G1

P500 pre-orders for every player

In progress
What we're solving"Being able to accept a P500 pre-order that does not require payment upfront is a requirement for everything." Jason Carr, October 30
The solution
  • GMT sells internationally through Shopify Markets: local currency, localized storefronts and per-market pricing from a single admin (Help Center).
  • The P500 app keeps the card on file and charges when the game ships. Shopify's pre-order rules still apply: customers can't use local payment methods for pre-orders, and the supported gateways include Shopify Payments (shopify.dev).
Confirmed on 10/30
  • Shopify Markets, not Managed Markets, so international players pre-order with nothing charged upfront
  • A $0 pre-order creates a real Shopify order, tagged P500 and visible in the customer's account
  • The price locks at the time of order. Later price changes only apply to new orders
  • Mixed carts split into separate orders, with messaging at checkout and in the confirmation email
  • Auto-orders follow designer and series rules
  • Warranty covers 90 days plus the first quarterly cycle

G2

Taxes handled and filed, international and US

Open
What we're solving"I don't feel comfortable moving forward with a solution and saying we'll figure out tax filing for the US later." Jason Carr, October 30
The solution
  • What Markets does on its own: calculates and collects duties and import taxes at checkout. GMT stays the merchant of record, so remitting and filing still need a partner (Help Center).
  • International: Zonos Duty and Tax "calculates guaranteed duties, taxes, and fees in your checkout." Per its listing, "Zonos remits VAT for you," with "no need to register for IOSS or obtain a UK VAT ID," and "Zonos pays any discrepancies on the customs bill" (Shopify App Store).
  • US: Shopify Tax calculates sales tax, and a filing partner files the returns. Praella recommended partners such as Numeral and offered introductions.
  • Every product needs an HS code and country of origin so duties calculate correctly.
Confirmed on 10/30
  • GMT doesn't need to be the merchant of record, as long as taxes and duties are handled and filed
  • Both international and US filing need a vetted answer before implementation starts
Still to come
  • ShopifyA vetted pick for international tax and duty remittance
  • PraellaIntroductions to US filing partners
Open questions
  • Which countries Zonos remits for beyond the EU and UK (for example Canada and Australia), and where GMT would still need its own registration
  • How Zonos handles a P500 order: are duties locked in at pre-order, or recalculated when the order is charged?
  • How Zonos shows duties at checkout: as a separate line, or built into the shipping price

G3

Lower shipping rates, proven with real numbers

Waiting on package data
What we're solving"Our customers are going to save a lot of money, but I can't put numbers around it." Jason Carr, October 30
The solution
  • Shopify Shipping: discounted labels from USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL Express and DHL eCommerce for US merchants (Help Center).
  • Printing at Hanford: "You can buy and print up to 250 shipping labels at the same time from the Orders page" (Help Center). No printing order by order.
  • Duties and labels: on orders where duties are collected at checkout, Shopify Shipping labels are limited to carriers that support duties-paid shipping, such as DHL Express. The rate comparison needs to cover those carriers.
  • ShipStation: Checkout Rates shows live rates at checkout for domestic and international orders, works with Shopify, and is "available at no extra cost" (ShipStation). It only wins if its rates beat GMT's current UPS contract.
Confirmed on 10/30
  • Every order ships from Hanford, including Australia
  • Fulfillment flexibility isn't the goal right now
  • Test the Shopify admin label workflow for real, with ShipStation as the backup
  • GMT's own UPS rates running through ShipStation isn't a win

Shopify Markets vs. Managed Markets

Both run on the same Shopify store. Markets is the foundation every international store uses. Managed Markets adds Global-e on top as the merchant of record. It doesn't let international customers place pre-orders, and P500 depends on them. Here's what else changes.
